Intimacy is a deep sense of closeness and connection that goes far beyond the physical, encompassing emotional
             openness, intellectual sharing, shared experiences, and aligned values; it is built on trust, honest
communication, vulnerability, respect for boundaries, and consistency over time, and it flourishes in
relationships where people feel safe, truly seen, and respected while still honoring each others individual
autonomy.

Intimacy** is a deep sense of closeness and connection between people. It’s not just physical— it includes
several dimensions:

- **Emotional** – sharing feelings, fears, hopes; being truly seen
- **Physical** – affection, touch, sexual closeness (when applicable)
- **Intellectual** – sharing ideas, curiosity, respectful debate
- **Experiential** – bonding through shared activities or goals
- **Spiritual** – aligned values, meaning, or sense of purpose

**Key ingredients**
- Trust & safety
- Honest communication
- Vulnerability (risking openness)
- Respect for boundaries
- Consistency over time
